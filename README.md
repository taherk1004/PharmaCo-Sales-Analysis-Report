# 💊 PharmaCo Sales Analysis Report

A end-to-end pharmaceutical sales analysis project built using **Microsoft Excel** and **Power BI**. The dataset was manually created from scratch using Excel shortcuts, and then visualized through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 📈 Dashboard Visuals

The Power BI dashboard includes the following charts:

1. **Revenue by Product** – Pie chart showing each product's revenue contribution
2. **Revenue by Region** – Pie chart breaking down sales across 5 regions
3. **COGS & Revenue Comparison** – Bar chart comparing cost vs revenue per product
4. **Units Sold by Product** – Bar chart of total units sold per product
5. **Revenue by Channel** – Bar chart across 5 sales channels
6. **Revenue by Month** – Monthly revenue trend across the full year

---

## 🔑 Key Insights

- **Oncoshield 100mg** dominates revenue at **62.6%** of total sales
- **Government** channel leads in revenue among all sales channels
- Revenue peaked in **February** and **November**
- **East** and **West** regions are the top-performing regions
